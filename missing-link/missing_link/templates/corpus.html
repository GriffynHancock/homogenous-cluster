What the columns are for. Chunks is what worker.chunk_spans actually produces at the current {{ chunk_tokens }}-token default — a document yielding fewer than {{ min_chunks }} chunks has no internal boundary and cannot exercise chunk-boundary behaviour at all. Clause markers is the fraction of sentences carrying unless / except / provided that / or until / whichever later / subject to / other than / apart from — the single number that says whether a document can speak to the severed-clause question (measured: 15.4% on legal-styled text vs 0.03% on narrative prose). Numbers/1k is checkable figures per 1000 words; a document with none cannot exercise the faithfulness cascade's hard number tier.
